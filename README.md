## 🌟 Why ScreenPen?

ScreenPen is a powerful, lightweight, and high-performance screen annotation suite built natively for macOS. Designed for lecturers, developers, and presenters who need a reliable way to guide their audience's attention in real-time.

**This is the "Offline Activation" version**, distributed outside the App Store for users who prefer machine-locked permanent licenses without Apple ID dependencies.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🖋️ Professional Annotation
- **Zero-Latency Drawing**: GPU-accelerated rendering using Apple's QuartzCore.
- **Precision Shapes**: Perfect Lines, Arrows, Rectangles, and Circles.
- **Smart Highlighter**: Semi-transparent overlays that preserve background legibility.
- **Mosaic Privacy**: Quickly blur sensitive information on your screen.

### 💎 PRO Productivity (Exclusives)
- **Dynamic Step Counter**: One-click numbered badges (1, 2, 3...) that automatically re-order when one is deleted. Perfect for tutorials.
- **SF Symbol Stamps**: Drop 20+ professional icons (Checkmarks, Stars, Warnings) with adaptive coloring.
- **Advanced Selection**: Move, edit, and adjust existing annotations fluidly.
- **Presentation Suite**: 
  *   **Laser Pointer**: Persistent trail for tracking movement.
  *   **Spotlight**: Focus attention on specific areas.
  *   **Live Magnifier**: Real-time zoom-in for fine details.
  *   **Keystroke Caster**: Visualize your keyboard shortcuts for the audience.
